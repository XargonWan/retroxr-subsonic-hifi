Subsonic Hi-Fi NAVI-AZ95 (xenu.subsonic)

A hi-fi CD changer for the RetroXR room that plays your own music server and internet radio.

  - Music server: anything that speaks the Subsonic API (https://www.subsonic.org/pages/api.jsp), tested with
    Navidrome (https://www.navidrome.org). Browse artists, albums and tracks on the unit's screen, search, play.
  - Internet radio: Icecast / Shoutcast style streams and playlists, tested with AzuraCast
    (https://www.azuracast.com), whose now-playing information the unit reads. Add your own stations from the
    unit's TAB menu.
  - A size-capped local cache of the tracks you play (default 50 MB, set from the TAB menu).
  - Its jacks are those of RetroXR's own CD player: LINE OUT L/R on the back, PHONES on the front, its own
    mains cord.

"Subsonic Hi-Fi NAVI-AZ95" is the in-game make. Subsonic, Navidrome and AzuraCast are the names of the
protocol and the projects this mod talks to; the mod is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

Status: first playable build. Netplay is not wired; the headset, the Quest and a long listen are still to be tried.
